    The USACE Greers Ferry Lake page lists 31,500 acres and 272 miles of shoreline at conservation pool (343 miles at flood pool, 487 ft). Published figures differ by source and survey method.

    Depth

    No public depth survey exists for Greers Ferry Lake (checked AGFC, USGS, USACE). Lidar elevation stops at the water surface. To see the old valley floor, use Pre-lake valley (1894) or USGS historic topo: rough depth = current lake level − the contour elevation.
